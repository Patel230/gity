# Nexus roadmap

1. Contract: vocabulary, graph schema, evidence, confidence, and safety.
2. Baseline graph: organizations, repositories, forks, exact imports, CI, and
   activity.
3. Nexus shell: Overview, Architecture, Repositories, and Explore.
4. Progressive architecture: domains, clustering, focus, breadcrumbs, and
   edge explanations.
5. Service discovery without assuming one repository equals one service.
6. Contracts and connections: APIs, events, queues, databases, caches, and
   external services.
7. Infrastructure and deployment locations.
8. Real use-case flows with animated playback.
9. Incremental indexing, stale state, manual refresh, webhooks, and schedules.
10. Documentation context and drift detection.
11. AI architecture Q&A over the normalized graph.
12. Scale, permissions, auditability, and performance hardening.
